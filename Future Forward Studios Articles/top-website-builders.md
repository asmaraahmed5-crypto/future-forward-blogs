# Top Website Builders in 2026: Compared by Price and Use

The top website builders serve different jobs: start with Wix or Squarespace for a business-site shortlist, Shopify for an online store, and WordPress.com for regular publishing. Consider Webflow or Framer when visual design is central to the project, then compare the plan needed for your actual pages and features.

## Quick picks

### Choose by the work you need to do

These picks are editorial recommendations based on official product information. They are not claims about hands-on tests, measured performance, or which editor every beginner will find easiest.

- **Small service website:** Investigate Wix and Squarespace for presenting services, work, and a clear enquiry path.
- **Product-selling business:** Investigate Shopify when products, checkout, and order management are the main job.
- **Article-led website:** Start your research with WordPress.com if you expect to publish guides regularly.
- **Design-focused project:** Compare Webflow and Framer around layout control, content structure, and who will maintain the result.
- **No initial subscription:** Compare the free options from Wix, WordPress.com, Webflow, and Framer, paying attention to their restrictions.

Do not read the order as a universal ranking. A tool that suits a retailer may add unnecessary complexity to a consultant’s information site.

### Decide your primary visitor action

Write one sentence describing what a visitor should do: request a quote, buy a product, read a guide, or view a portfolio. Use that action to decide which tools deserve a closer look.

For example, a fictional florist needs a different setup depending on whether visitors enquire about custom arrangements or purchase listed products. The business category alone does not settle the platform choice.

## Reviews of each builder

### Wix: a starting point for a service business

Wix provides free website creation and publishing with templates and design tools. Its free site uses a Wix address, while an upgrade is needed to connect your own domain and remove Wix advertising. [Wix free website guide](https://support.wix.com/en/article/building-a-website-for-free).

Our suggested use case is a small business that wants to organize its services and build a first version visually. Start with a homepage, service page, About page, and working contact method.

**What makes it worth considering:** You can prepare a live first version before committing to a paid subscription. Use that stage to learn which sections help customers and which template elements can be removed.

**What to watch:** Identify the plan required by the business action you need. A service description and a request for online payment are different requirements, so do not assume they belong to the same tier.

Wix lists Light as its basic paid offering, with 2 GB storage and two site collaborators. Check its full plan comparison before adding integrations or inviting more people to manage the site. [Wix paid plans](https://www.wix.com/plans).

**Our recommendation:** Put Wix on the shortlist for an enquiry-led business site. Judge it by how comfortably the owner can update service information and maintain the contact journey.

If you want a free first build, use [our step-by-step free website guide](https://futureforwardstudios.com/create-a-website-for-free/). Plan for an upgrade when a specific restriction affects the business.

### Squarespace: investigate it for presentation-led sites

Squarespace offers website templates, portfolio and blogging products, and commerce-related services. Its subscriptions are billed monthly or annually, and its official FAQ describes a 14-day trial. [Squarespace overview and FAQ](https://www.squarespace.com/about/faq).

Our suggested use case is a business with strong existing content: photographs, examples of work, clear service descriptions, and a defined next step. Consider it when presenting those materials coherently is a priority.

**What makes it worth considering:** The platform brings website building and hosting into the same service. You can evaluate the editor and the presentation of your real material before subscribing.

**What to watch:** A trial gives you time to evaluate the product, but it does not establish a free long-term publishing arrangement. Decide whether the ongoing subscription fits the business before rebuilding a large archive.

Squarespace also explains that separate fees can apply to what you sell, and that annual billing can include an eligible domain for the first year. Compare the relevant plan rather than assuming every sales workflow has the same cost. [Squarespace plan selection](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/206536797-Choose-the-right-Squarespace-plan).

**Our recommendation:** Shortlist Squarespace for a portfolio, professional service, or other presentation-led website. During evaluation, change the sample copy and images immediately so the design is judged using the content you will actually publish.

### Shopify: choose around commerce operations

Shopify’s pricing page organizes its offering around selling online and managing a business, with Basic positioned for solo entrepreneurs. The displayed annual-billing Basic rate is US$29 per month, separate from introductory promotions. [Shopify pricing](https://www.shopify.com/pricing).

Our suggested use case is a website where customers need to browse products and complete purchases. Before comparing themes, map the journey from product selection to order confirmation.

**What makes it worth considering:** A commerce-centered platform is relevant when your repeated tasks involve products, orders, and payment decisions. Evaluate those tasks as a connected process rather than choosing from homepage screenshots alone.

**What to watch:** Payment processing and third-party transaction fees are different charges. Shopify explains that using Shopify Payments as the sole payment provider avoids its third-party transaction fees; other payment-related charges still need review. [Shopify Payments fees](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/onboarding/cost-of-shopify-payments).

Also list any required apps, themes, domain costs, or operational services. Their presence in the ecosystem does not mean every extra is included in the base subscription.

**Our recommendation:** Put Shopify first in the research process when selling products is the website’s main job. For a service business that only needs enquiries, establish whether a commerce-oriented setup provides benefits you will actually use.

### WordPress.com: a starting point for a publishing routine

WordPress.com’s free offering includes hosting, unlimited pages and posts, and 1 GB of media storage. Paid plans add capabilities such as plugin installation, so choose around the features your publication needs. [WordPress.com plan features](https://wordpress.com/support/plan-features/).

Our suggested use case is a website built around articles, guides, or an expanding archive. Test the repeated editorial tasks: creating a post, correcting an older page, adding images, and linking related content.

**What makes it worth considering:** The free plan does not impose a small fixed number of posts or pages. That makes it useful for exploring a publishing process before paying for additional capabilities.

**What to watch:** Free publishing is different from unrestricted control over a site. Work out the domain, integration, storage, and advertising requirements before choosing the long-term plan.

The Personal plan is shown at US$4 per month when paid yearly and US$9 month-to-month. Longer commitments show other monthly equivalents, so compare the full period rather than simply selecting the smallest advertised number. [WordPress.com pricing](https://wordpress.com/pricing/).

**Our recommendation:** Start here when content production is the central task. Make sure the person publishing articles can manage the routine independently rather than relying on help for every correction.

### Webflow: assess layout needs and plan structure together

Webflow’s free Starter plan includes two static pages, a Webflow.io address, 1 GB bandwidth, and 50 form submissions. Basic is shown at US$15 per month billed yearly and is described as a plan for sites that do not need a CMS. [Webflow pricing](https://webflow.com/pricing).

Our suggested use case is a design-focused project where the owner or builder wants to work closely with layouts. It also belongs on a learning shortlist if understanding that design workflow is part of your goal.

**What makes it worth considering:** You can explore a small project without buying a Site plan immediately. Keep the first experiment within its limits so a promising design does not depend on pages the free plan cannot publish.

**What to watch:** Site plans and Workspace plans serve different purposes. A paid Workspace is not a substitute for the Site plan required to publish on a custom domain.

Webflow lists code export as a paid Workspace capability. It also distinguishes exported static files from dynamic CMS content, which is not included in that export. [Webflow Workspace comparison](https://webflow.com/pricing).

**Our recommendation:** Consider Webflow when layout control and the chosen maintenance workflow justify the setup effort. For a growing article site, compare the appropriate CMS-capable plan rather than assuming Basic covers the publishing requirement.

### Framer: consider it for a visual project

Framer lists a Free plan and a Basic plan shown at $10 per month with annual billing selected. Basic includes a custom-domain option, 30 site pages, two CMS collections, and 50 GB bandwidth. [Framer pricing](https://www.framer.com/pricing).

Our suggested use case is a visual personal site or small project whose requirements fit those boundaries. Use your own photographs and text while exploring it, because template content can hide layout issues.

**What makes it worth considering:** A free starting option lets you investigate the design process before subscribing. Focus on whether you can make ordinary changes without repeatedly disturbing the surrounding layout.

**What to watch:** Free sites use a Framer domain and display platform branding. Custom-domain publishing requires payment, so factor that into a professional business-site budget. [Framer plan guidance](https://www.framer.com/help/articles/best-use-cases-for-each-framer-plan/).

Framer does not provide HTML export for self-hosting. If moving the complete website to another host is an essential requirement, account for that before committing. [Framer export documentation](https://www.framer.com/help/articles/can-i-export-my-website-to-html-and-self-host-it/).

**Our recommendation:** Compare Framer with Webflow for a visual project, but use your actual page count, content structure, and future maintenance needs to choose between them.

## How to choose

### Compare the full first-year and renewal costs

List the required website plan, domain, business email, paid apps, and any payment-related charges. Distinguish mandatory costs from optional extras.

Use the same billing period for every candidate. A monthly equivalent on an annual subscription is not the same payment arrangement as a cancel-any-month subscription.

For a simple illustration, a US$15 monthly equivalent billed yearly represents US$180 for twelve months before taxes or extras. That calculation does not establish the renewal price or guarantee the same terms in another country.

### Check what website builder hosting includes

These platforms combine publishing with their own hosting arrangements. For a site hosted on the selected service, you generally choose the relevant platform plan rather than purchasing unrelated hosting separately.

Keep domain registration and email separate in your budget. Also investigate backups, exports, and recovery so you understand what can be restored or moved later.

### Try the work you will repeat

Build one representative page and make a realistic change. Replace an image, edit navigation, and check the result on a phone.

For a store, include a product and the purchase journey. For a publication, include an article with headings, links, and media.

If you are wondering what is the best web builder, this exercise is more useful than comparing feature counts. The best website creator for your business should make essential work manageable for the person responsible for updates.

### Make ownership and handover clear

Use an account controlled by the business, and decide who is responsible for billing and renewals. If someone builds the site for you, make sure the handover covers ordinary updates rather than just delivery of a finished homepage.

Ask the future owner to change a service description and correct a menu label using the supplied instructions. If those tasks are confusing, improve the instructions or reconsider the workflow before the site becomes larger.

Keep original text, photographs, and other important assets in an organized location outside the editor. That gives the business reusable source material even when an entire design cannot be exported.

Finally, write down what must be checked regularly: contact delivery, product information, service details, and access permissions. A useful website needs ongoing ownership after the initial build is complete.

### Keep the first version focused

Do not buy an elaborate system because you might need every feature someday. Choose a platform that meets your immediate requirements and has a plausible route to the next stage.

For a service-led launch, follow [how to create a free business website](https://futureforwardstudios.com/free-business-website/). If avoiding a subscription is the priority, compare [the free website makers](https://futureforwardstudios.com/best-free-website-makers/) before committing.

Among the top website builders, the right choice is the one that supports your business model, fits the complete budget, and remains manageable after launch. Choose for the work your website needs to do, then improve the design as you learn.

## FAQ

### What is the best website builder for a small business?

Start with Wix or Squarespace for a service-site shortlist, and Shopify when the main job is selling products. These are use-case recommendations; your required features, budget, and maintenance process should decide the final choice.

### Which website builder is best for blogging?

WordPress.com is our suggested starting point because its free plan supports unlimited posts and pages. Check storage, customization, and monetization requirements before choosing the plan for a growing publication.

### Can I build a website for free in 2026?

Yes: Wix, WordPress.com, Webflow, and Framer document free options, each with restrictions. Squarespace offers a trial rather than a continuing free website plan, and Shopify’s introductory offer should not be confused with permanent free hosting.

### Do website builders include hosting and a domain?

The platforms in this comparison provide hosting for sites published through their services, but domain arrangements depend on the plan and offer. Check domain registration, renewal, connection requirements, and email separately before purchasing.
