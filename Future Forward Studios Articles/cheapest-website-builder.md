# Cheapest Website Builders in 2026: Real Costs Compared

The cheapest website builder depends on the website you need and the period you are paying for: Carrd is worth considering for a compact landing page, while WordPress.com belongs on a low-cost publishing shortlist. Compare the upfront payment, renewal terms, domain expenses, and required features before treating a low monthly headline as the final cost.

### What “renewal cost” means here

Some providers publish a specific post-promotion rate; others provide a list price and show your actual renewal amount inside the account. This table distinguishes those situations instead of assuming the opening price lasts forever.

WordPress.com explains that promotional discounts apply to the initial subscription period and that renewal charges are available in the billing area. Check the relevant purchase before using the current annual price as your second-year budget. [WordPress.com sales FAQ](https://wordpress.com/support/sales-faq/).

Carrd’s annual price and Framer’s annual-billing rate describe their current offers. Neither figure should be read as a promise that future renewals can never change.

### Calculate what leaves your account today

For an annual plan, multiply the advertised monthly equivalent by twelve. For a four-year commitment, multiply it by forty-eight, then compare that amount with what you can reasonably spend now.

A lower equivalent rate can still require the larger payment. Separate that cash-flow question from the question of which plan is cheaper across a longer period.

## Builders under $10 a month: finding the cheapest website builder

### Carrd Pro Standard: consider it for a focused page

Carrd’s official documentation lists Pro Standard at US$19 per year, including custom-domain publishing, forms, embeds, and removal of Carrd branding. It also lists a cheaper Pro Lite plan at US$9 per year, but Lite does not include the same custom-domain and form capabilities. [Carrd plan details](https://carrd.com/docs/pro/plans).

That distinction matters for a business. Choosing the smallest price is unhelpful if you then discover that your enquiry method or domain requirement belongs to another tier.

Pro Standard is our suggested starting point when evaluating Carrd for a compact business presence. Budget for the domain itself separately rather than interpreting support for a custom address as free domain registration.

Imagine a fictional freelance consultant whose first site needs an introduction, three service summaries, selected work, and a contact action. A focused page can present that material without creating an elaborate website structure.

Before choosing this route, sketch how the content will grow. If the real plan is an expanding library of guides, use the publishing workflow as your deciding requirement rather than the annual subscription alone.

### WordPress.com Personal: consider it for publishing

WordPress.com lists Personal at US$4 per month on an annual commitment and US$9 on month-to-month billing. It also displays lower monthly equivalents for longer terms, so the advertised rate needs to be read with its billing period. [WordPress.com pricing](https://wordpress.com/pricing/).

Personal is worth investigating if you need service information alongside regular articles. Choose a representative post and consider how you will add headings, media, links, and future revisions.

The broader WordPress.com plan documentation includes hosting and unlimited pages and posts, including on the free version. This helps distinguish a publishing-oriented service from a plan with a small fixed page allowance. [WordPress.com plan features](https://wordpress.com/support/plan-features/).

Do not treat a domain’s included first year as a permanent saving. Record the separate domain renewal alongside the website subscription, and check any email or additional service you intend to use.

Our recommendation is based on the publishing use case, not a claim that it produces better rankings. A manageable editorial routine matters more than buying a plan and leaving the site unmaintained.

### Hostinger Premium: compare the long commitment carefully

The researched offer shows Premium at US$1.99 per month when US$95.52 is paid for forty-eight months. It explicitly shows renewal at US$10.99 per month for the same term. [Hostinger builder pricing](https://www.hostinger.com/website-builder).

At that displayed renewal rate, forty-eight months would total US$527.52 before applicable extras or taxes. That is arithmetic from the advertised term, not a separate checkout quote.

The offer may appeal to someone with a settled long-term project. It deserves more caution when you are still deciding whether the business, website structure, or platform fits.

Hostinger also lists the included domain and mailboxes as free for the first year. Build later charges into your comparison rather than spreading the opening subscription across four years and ignoring the extras.

Before buying, write down the first payment and the next large renewal date. A low monthly equivalent is easier to assess when the actual payment schedule is visible.

### Framer Basic: a useful budget boundary

Framer Basic is shown at US$10 per month with yearly billing selected. It belongs at the edge of this comparison rather than being described as strictly under US$10. [Framer pricing](https://www.framer.com/pricing).

Consider it when a visually focused site fits your needs and its overall cost is acceptable. Do not choose it solely because the opening template looks more polished than your unfinished content elsewhere.

Framer bills additional editors separately from the Site plan. Its billing guide also explains that annual subscriptions are paid upfront and renew automatically on the anniversary date unless canceled. [Framer subscription charges](https://www.framer.com/help/articles/subscriptions-and-charges/).

A solo owner and a team with several editing accounts therefore need different budgets. Check who genuinely needs edit access before treating the base site price as the complete total.

## Hidden fees

### Domain registration and renewal

Separate domain connection from domain ownership. A builder may let you attach an address without including its registration, while an introductory bundle may include only the first year.

For Webador’s researched UK offer, the domain becomes £20 per year after the included first year. That gives a concrete example of a charge that sits outside the headline website rate. [Webador domain terms](https://www.webador.co.uk/pricing).

Record the renewal date and the account responsible for paying it. Keeping the website subscription active does not substitute for renewing a separately billed domain.

### Email and third-party services

Do not assume a website subscription includes every service your business needs. List business email, appointment tools, newsletter software, and other required subscriptions separately.

For each service, ask whether it is essential at launch, whether an existing tool already handles the task, and what happens when a trial ends. Remove optional items from the initial budget until they solve a specific problem.

### Payment costs for online selling

If you are searching for the best free ecommerce website builder, define what “free” needs to cover. Building a catalog, publishing a store, accepting payments, and fulfilling orders are different parts of the operation.

A plan with no extra platform transaction charge may still involve a payment processor and other business expenses. Request the applicable payment terms before assuming that every sale reaches your account without deductions.

Build a store budget around your products and payment method. A low website subscription is only one component of running an online shop.

### Paid seats, resources, and feature upgrades

Ask which plan supports the complete workflow. A cheap site becomes more expensive if a required feature forces an upgrade or each additional editor introduces another recurring charge.

Make a short must-have list before comparing plans. For a service business, that might include a domain, an appropriate contact method, and the ability for the owner to update service details.

Keep optional design effects outside that list. Paying more for decoration is a separate choice from paying to make the business process work.

### Taxes, currency, and commitment length

Check the currency and tax treatment on the actual checkout. Comparing a UK rate excluding VAT with a US dollar figure does not establish which option costs less for your account.

Also distinguish stopping automatic renewal from receiving a refund. Read the cancellation and refund terms for the selected commitment before paying, especially when the term is several years.

### Moving and rebuilding later

Consider how you would leave before the site becomes large. Find out which content can be exported and which parts would need rebuilding, then keep your original text and images organized independently.

This is a planning question, not a reason to avoid every hosted platform. A useful exit path lets you make an informed choice about convenience now and possible work later.

## Which cheap plan is worth it

### Match the plan to a specific website

For a focused landing page, investigate Carrd Pro Standard first. For an article-led site, put WordPress.com on the shortlist and evaluate the publishing process with your own sample content.

For a settled long-term project, examine Hostinger’s prepaid offer alongside its explicitly higher renewal rate. Consider Framer when the visual workflow justifies its base price and any collaboration costs.

These are starting recommendations, not a universal ranking. The best cheap website builder for one business can be an awkward fit for another.

### Compare two complete budgets

Create one budget for the first commitment and another for the next renewal period. Include the website subscription, domain, necessary services, and any required upgrades in both.

Keep the periods clear. A twelve-month renewal and a forty-eight-month renewal cannot be compared fairly by looking only at the amount charged on the day.

Calculate a monthly equivalent to understand the rate, then check whether the actual upfront payment fits your cash flow. Both views are useful, but neither replaces the other.

### Confirm the workflow before paying

Build a representative page and try an ordinary update. Change a paragraph, replace an image, and check the result on a phone.

For a business site, test the contact action. For a publication, add a second article and decide whether the process is manageable enough to repeat.

Follow [our free business website walkthrough](https://futureforwardstudios.com/free-business-website/) if you need a clear starting sequence. For a broader shortlist beyond low-cost plans, see [top website builders compared](https://futureforwardstudios.com/top-website-builders/).

### Use a simple buying worksheet

For each finalist, write the amount payable today, the length of service it covers, and the next expected payment date. Add a separate line for every required service that has a different renewal schedule.

Then write one sentence explaining why the plan qualifies: “It supports our service pages, business address, and enquiry method.” If you cannot complete that sentence confidently, investigate the missing requirement before buying.

Keep a copy of the offer and checkout summary with the date. That record helps you distinguish the terms you accepted from an advertisement that changes later.

Finally, assign one person to review renewals before they are due. A budget is easier to manage when the business knows which account owns each subscription and who can change or cancel it.

### Spend when the benefit is clear

An affordable website builder should support the work you need without creating unnecessary commitments. Pay more when you can name the benefit, such as a suitable domain setup, a required integration, or a simpler maintenance process.

The cheapest website builder is the lowest-cost option that actually meets those requirements across the period you expect to use it. Choose using full payments and renewal terms, then keep the first version focused on useful information and a working visitor journey.

## FAQ

### What is the cheapest website builder for a small business?

Carrd Pro Standard is a low-cost starting point for a compact business presence at its published US$19 annual rate. For a business expecting regular articles or a larger structure, compare WordPress.com and the other options against the complete feature requirements.

### Which website builders cost less than US$10 a month?

Carrd Pro Standard and WordPress.com Personal fall below that threshold on the billing terms described above, while Hostinger’s researched Premium promotion also does so during its initial term. Framer Basic is exactly US$10 on the displayed annual-billing rate, and Hostinger’s published Premium renewal is above US$10.

### Do cheap website builders increase their prices at renewal?

Some introductory offers have an explicitly higher renewal rate, as the Hostinger and Webador examples show. Other services provide a list price or an account-specific renewal amount, so check the actual subscription terms rather than assuming either an increase or a permanent price.

### Is a free website cheaper than a paid plan?

A free plan avoids an initial website subscription when its limits fit your needs. If you need paid domain connection, additional features, or a different monetization setup, compare the complete required arrangement rather than the word “free.”
