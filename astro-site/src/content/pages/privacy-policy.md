# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is handled when you visit futureforwardstudios.com (the "site"), how it is used, and what choices you have. It is a draft prepared for the site owner and is not legal advice.

**Last updated: November 17, 2026.**

## Who we are

The site is operated by Future Forward Studios ("we", "us"). We operate from the United States.

You can contact us about this policy at [contactus@futureforwardstudios.com](mailto:contactus@futureforwardstudios.com).

## Information we collect

### Information you give us

The site currently has no accounts, comments, or sign-up forms. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to reply to you.

If we add a newsletter, contact form, comments, or user accounts in the future, we will update this policy before they go live to explain what each one collects and why.

### Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the server that hosts the site may record technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you request, and the date and time. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure, and diagnose problems.

The site is hosted and delivered through Cloudflare, Inc., a provider based in the United States. Cloudflare processes visitor data such as IP addresses and request details to serve pages, protect the site from attacks, and keep it running. We do not keep our own separate copy of these logs. How long Cloudflare retains this data is set by Cloudflare; see the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for details.

### Cookies and similar technologies

The site's own pages do not set cookies. We use Google Tag Manager (see below), which loads the measurement tags we configure, and those tags may set cookies or use similar identifiers in your browser.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can use browser privacy controls or Google's [Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) to limit measurement. If we add advertising services later, we will name them, describe the data they collect, and add the required consent options before they go live.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- operate, secure, and improve the site;
- understand which articles are useful;
- reply to messages you send us; and
- meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Third-party services

We may link to other websites, such as the platforms discussed in our guides. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

**Google Tag Manager.** The site loads Google Tag Manager, a tag management service provided by Google LLC. When a page loads, your browser contacts Google, which receives technical data such as your IP address, device and browser details, and the page visited. Tag Manager then runs whichever measurement tags we have set up in it, for example website analytics, which help us understand which guides are read. Google's handling of this data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The site does not currently embed social media or third-party media. If we introduce display advertising in the future, advertising partners may use cookies to show and measure ads, and we will explain this here and link to opt-out controls such as [Your Online Choices](https://www.youronlinechoices.com/) and the [Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out](https://optout.aboutads.info/).

## Sharing information

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site (for example, hosting), when the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of the site, its users, or others.

## How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above. Server logs are retained by Cloudflare under its own policies, as described above. Emails you send us are kept for as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of the conversation, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to certain uses, or to withdraw consent. To make a request, contact us using the details above. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

We are based in the United States, and our visitors may come from anywhere. Residents of some US states, such as California, and of regions such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, have additional privacy rights. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We will respond to any rights request sent to the email address above within the time the applicable law requires.

## Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the site, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date will show when it last changed, and the current version will always be on this page.
