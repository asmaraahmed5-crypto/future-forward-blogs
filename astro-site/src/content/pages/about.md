# About Future Forward Studios

<img src="/logo-primary.png" width="96" height="96" alt="Future Forward Studios logo" class="about-logo">

Future Forward Studios publishes practical, step-by-step guides for people building things online. The guides are written for beginners and focus on what to do, what to check, and when spending money makes sense.

## What we publish

Our articles cover getting started with websites and online projects. Each guide aims to give you a clear plan, an honest view of the limits, and a checklist you can follow without prior experience.

We try to write for one reader at a time: someone who has a goal, limited time, and no wish to wade through jargon.

## How we write our guides

- **One purpose per article.** Each guide answers a specific question and says plainly who it is for.
- **Checked details.** Prices, plan limits, and product features change. We verify them against current official documentation before an article is published.
- **Real experience.** Where a guide describes something we did ourselves, we say so and show it. We do not claim to have tested a product we have not used.
- **No invented claims.** We avoid unsupported statistics, fake reviews, and exaggerated promises.

Future Forward Studios is a small, independent publisher working in the United States. We write each guide to be useful to someone starting from scratch, and we keep this site free of clutter so the information is easy to find and follow.

## Independence and how the site is funded

The site currently runs no advertising and uses no affiliate links. If that changes, we will say so clearly here and in our Privacy Policy and Terms, and label any relevant content.

## Corrections and updates

Mistakes happen and products change. If you spot something out of date or incorrect, please tell us and we will review it.

To report a correction or ask a question, email [contactus@futureforwardstudios.com](mailto:contactus@futureforwardstudios.com).
